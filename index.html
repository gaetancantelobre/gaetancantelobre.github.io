3D-Printed 1.2-Meter Sub-250g Delta Wing

Avionics

I love delta wings. I designed a 1.2-meter, sub-250-gram (232g AUW) RC delta wing running on 2S Li-Po batteries. The airfoil and wing geometry were designed in OpenVSP, with the fuselage and elevon control surfaces modeled in Autodesk Fusion 360.

The airframe was printed in foaming Lightweight PLA (LW-PLA), keeping the full wing panels (for a 500mm chord span) down to approximately 100 grams. With an aspect ratio of 5.4, the aircraft functions efficiently as a thermal glider. Structural rigidity is reinforced using six aluminum tubes as internal spars and truss rods.

While 3D-printed planes are brittle during hard landings, this build proved how viable ultra-lightweight printed airframes can be.

Tilt Indicator for Competitive Shooting

Hardware Software

Designed for competitive target shooting, this digital 3-axis electronic level provides rapid visual feedback to prevent canting errors. The project has undergone continuous refinement across four hardware iterations: from an initial Arduino Nano (ATmega328P) prototype to an ESP32-C3, STM8, and ultimately an STM32G4 architecture.

The system evolved from through-hole LEDs to magnetic modular boards equipped with addressable WS2812B LEDs, delivering a precision readout down to a quarter of a degree (0.25°).

Sub-100g Endurance Delta Wing

Avionics

Can a sub-100-gram aircraft fly for over an hour? This proof-of-concept project features a 40cm wingspan delta wing weighing just ~93 grams all-up weight (AUW). By capitalizing on the high energy density of an 18650 Li-ion cell (102.9 mAh/g vs. 33.3 mAh/g for equivalent Li-Po), the aircraft achieved remarkable flight endurance in an ultra-micro category.

3 DOF Space Mouse for CAD Navigation

Hardware

Commercial 3D space mice for CAD modeling in Autodesk Fusion 360 are notoriously expensive. To create an ergonomic, accessible alternative, I designed a 3-Degree-of-Freedom (3 DOF) controller powered by a Raspberry Pi Pico.

Version 1 implemented an Adafruit LIS3MDL 3-axis magnetometer suspended on mechanical springs over a permanent magnet, translating tilt, pan, and rotation into smooth CAD camera navigation. Version 2 refined the ergonomics with tactile analog inputs and rotary encoding.

TWIM IMPULSE - Custom AZERTY Keyboard

Hardware

Born from a practical everyday need: as a French typist who uses 60% keyboards, virtually all custom PCB kits are restricted to physical QWERTY layouts. To stop juggling incompatible physical and virtual layouts between home and university, I designed a true native AZERTY 60% keyboard from schematic to firmware.

Driven by an ATmega32U4 MCU, the board bypassed monolithic QMK builds in favor of lightweight, fully customized firmware utilizing TinyUSB_Mouse_and_Keyboard. This unlocked low-level control of HID endpoints and custom macro logic within 32 KB of flash memory. Designed in KiCad with custom silkscreen artwork and produced by JLCPCB.
